# Veylo — manual fulfilment guide (Phase 1)

When a Stripe payment comes through, follow these steps to place the supplier order on AliExpress.

---

## Step 1 — Check the Stripe notification

You'll get an email from Stripe with the customer's name, product(s) ordered, and shipping address. Note these down.

## Step 2 — Open the right AliExpress listing

**Veylo Wand** → https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005006107361063.html
- Select variant: **Pink with Box** or **Silver with Box** (match the finish the customer chose — Pearl = Silver, Blush = Pink)
- Price: ~£12.48

**Relief Belt** → https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005008660925538.html
- Select variant: **UK Plug**
- Price: ~£23.80

**IR Panel** (if approved) → https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005008715945024.html
- Select variant: **UK Plug**
- Price: ~£64.50

**Cool Roller / Sculpt Set** — supplier links TBD (not yet in ad campaigns)

## Step 3 — Enter the customer's shipping address

At AliExpress checkout, enter the customer's delivery address exactly as Stripe shows it. Double-check the postcode.

## Step 4 — Add a note to the seller

In the "Leave a message to the seller" box, write:

> This is a gift. Please do not include any invoice, price tag, or promotional materials in the package.

This prevents the customer seeing the supplier price.

## Step 5 — Pay and save the order number

Pay with your card. Screenshot or note the AliExpress order number.

## Step 6 — Get tracking and email the customer

Once AliExpress shows a tracking number (usually 1–3 days), send the customer a brief email:

> Subject: Your Veylo order is on its way
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Your [product name] has been dispatched and is on its way to you. You can expect delivery within 4–7 working days.
>
> Tracking: [number] (track at 17track.net)
>
> Thanks for choosing Veylo.

---

## Bundle orders (Glow Ritual / Relief Ritual / Relief Duo)

These are two separate AliExpress orders shipped to the same address. Place both, then email the customer with both tracking numbers once available.

- **Glow Ritual** = Wand + Cool Roller → two separate orders
- **Relief Ritual** = Wand + Belt → two separate orders
- **Relief Duo** = Belt × 2 → two separate AliExpress orders for the same Belt listing (max 1/order)

---

## Important notes

- **Max 1 per order** on all three confirmed suppliers. If a customer orders 2 of the same product, you'll need to place two separate AliExpress orders (possibly from different seller accounts if the limit is per-buyer).
- **Delivery estimate on site says 4–7 working days** — this matches AliExpress quotes of Sep 02–07 from today. Monitor actual delivery times and adjust site copy if needed.
- **Keep a simple spreadsheet**: Stripe payment ID | Customer name | AliExpress order number(s) | Tracking number(s) | Delivery confirmed. This is your fulfilment log until CJ takes over.
- **Returns**: if a customer wants a return within 30 days, refund via Stripe. Don't try to return to AliExpress — eat the COGS. At 3× margin you can absorb occasional returns.
